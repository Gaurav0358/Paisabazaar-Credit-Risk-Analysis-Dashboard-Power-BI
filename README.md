## 📌 Project Overview
Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze banking customer data, credit risk indicators, and potential fraud patterns. The dashboard provides insights into customer profiles, credit scores, outstanding debt, EMI obligations, and high-risk customer segments to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze customer credit profiles and financial characteristics.
- Identify high-risk customers across occupations and age groups.
- Examine relationships between credit scores, monthly EMI, and payment delays.
- Monitor fraud-related indicators and credit utilization patterns.
- Enable interactive analysis using slicers and KPI cards.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI** – Interactive dashboard development
- **DAX** – KPI calculations and analytical measures
- **Data Modeling** – Organizing data for reporting
- **Data Visualization** – Charts, KPI cards, and interactive filters

## 📊 Key Dashboard Features

- **Customer Overview:** Total customers and total applications.
- **Financial KPIs:** Average annual income and average outstanding debt.
- **Credit Risk Analysis:** High-risk customer count by occupation and age group.
- **Credit Score Analysis:** Relationship between credit scores, EMI obligations, and payment delays.
- **Fraud Monitoring:** Fraud ratio and high-risk customer trends over time.
- **Credit Utilization:** Customer distribution across utilization categories.
- **Interactive Filters:** Filter the dashboard by age group, credit score, and income group.

## 💼 Business Value
The dashboard helps stakeholders explore credit risk indicators, understand customer financial behavior, and identify segments requiring further investigation. It supports more informed risk monitoring and reporting.

*Note: Dashboard indicators highlight potential risk patterns and should not be treated as definitive evidence of fraud.*

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Download the `.pbix` file from this repository.
2. Open it using Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. Interact with the KPI cards, charts, and slicers to explore the analysis.
